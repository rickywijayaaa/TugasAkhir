I · Ringkasan Eksekutif

TL;DR

Inti Eksperimen
Kita uji lima varian prompt generator pada empat metode RAG (baseline, QR, CR, QR+CR) menggunakan GPT-4.1-mini di 100 sampel pertama PubMedQA. Tujuan: menemukan bentuk prompt yang paling baik untuk metode tertentu, terutama untuk meningkatkan akurasi kelas maybe yang saat ini hanya 4.5 persen.

Yang akan dijawab

  • Apakah strict grounding tanpa larangan eksplisit terhadap "maybe" memperbaiki akurasi kelas maybe?
  • Apakah chain-of-thought meningkatkan faithfulness?
  • Apakah few-shot demonstrations lebih efektif dari zero-shot CoT?
  • Apakah structured causal reasoning ala MedCoT-RAG bersinergi dengan Context Reranking?

Output yang diharapkan

  • Rekomendasi prompt terbaik per metrik.
  • Rekomendasi pasangan prompt-metode paling sinergis.
  • Analisis mengapa prompt tertentu lebih efektif di setting biomedis tertutup.
II · Latar Belakang

Konteks dari Eksperimen Sebelumnya

Pada eksperimen utama TA (baseline OpenAI, 500 sampel), label accuracy mencapai 69.2 persen dengan distribusi per kelas yang sangat tidak seimbang.

KelasAkurasiJumlah SampelPrediksi Sistem

Temuan Kritis
Hanya 3 dari 66 sampel kelas maybe yang berhasil diprediksi benar. Penyebab utama: prompt baseline menulis "Do NOT use maybe simply because the evidence is limited". Instruksi ini menekan model menjauh dari kelas maybe walaupun bukti memang tidak konklusif.
II · Latar Belakang

Mengapa Prompt Generator yang Divariasi

Pada pipeline RAG terdapat tiga prompt yang bisa di-engineer: query rewriting, generator, dan evaluator. Eksperimen ini fokus ke generator prompt karena tiga alasan utama.

Alasan 1

Dampak Terbesar

Literatur menyebut sekitar 80 persen kegagalan RAG di produksi berasal dari desain prompt generator, bukan kualitas retrieval.

Alasan 2

Paling Cepat

Tidak perlu rebuild index BM25 atau ChromaDB. Tidak perlu ganti model embedding atau LLM. Cukup ubah string template.

Alasan 3

Hipotesis Spesifik

Setiap varian punya prediksi konkret terhadap metrik tertentu (akurasi, faithfulness, recall maybe). Bisa di-isolasi dampaknya.

Klaim Utama
Variasi pada prompt generator adalah intervensi paling cost-effective untuk mitigasi halusinasi pada konfigurasi RAG kita yang sudah ada.
II · Latar Belakang

Empat Hipotesis Utama (1 dari 2)

Setiap varian prompt dirancang untuk menguji satu hipotesis spesifik.

H1 · Anti-Maybe Bias

Prompt yang menghapus larangan eksplisit terhadap "maybe" akan meningkatkan recall kelas maybe tanpa menurunkan akurasi yes dan no secara signifikan.

Diuji oleh varian P1 (Strict Grounding + Balanced Maybe). Metrik kunci: akurasi maybe.

H2 · Chain-of-Thought

Prompt dengan langkah reasoning eksplisit akan meningkatkan faithfulness karena model dipaksa menyebutkan bukti sebelum menyimpulkan.

Diuji oleh varian P2 (Zero-shot CoT). Metrik kunci: faithfulness, context recall.

II · Latar Belakang

Empat Hipotesis Utama (2 dari 2)

H3 · Few-shot Anchoring

Demonstrasi 3 contoh (yes, no, maybe) akan menstabilkan format jawaban dan memperbaiki kasus borderline lebih baik daripada zero-shot CoT.

Diuji oleh varian P3 (Few-shot CoT). Metrik kunci: per-label accuracy, terutama maybe.

H4 · Sinergi Prompt-Metode

Prompt structured CoT ala MedCoT-RAG akan memberi gain paling besar saat dipasangkan dengan Context Reranking karena rerank menjamin kualitas konteks yang lebih tinggi.

Diuji oleh kombinasi P4 + CR. Metrik kunci: delta accuracy P4+CR vs P0+CR.

Kontribusi
Hasil eksperimen ini akan memberi panduan empiris tentang mana pola prompt engineering dari literatur umum yang ter-generalize ke domain biomedis tertutup PubMedQA.

IV · Varian 1 dari 5

P0 Baseline (Control)

Tujuan

Kontrol eksperimen. Prompt ini sama persis dengan yang dipakai di eksperimen utama TA. Hasilnya jadi titik bandingan untuk semua varian lain.

Karakteristik utama

  • Single-step instruction tanpa reasoning chain
  • Definisi maybe yang restriktif
  • Larangan eksplisit terhadap maybe
  • max_tokens output: 300

Ekspektasi hasil

Mereplikasi hasil baseline yang sudah ada:

  • Akurasi total ~69 persen
  • Akurasi yes ~88 persen
  • Akurasi no ~63 persen
  • Akurasi maybe ~5 persen (target perbaikan untuk P1-P4)

Diuji untuk hipotesis

Tidak menguji hipotesis. Berperan sebagai baseline pembanding.

P0 Baseline (Prompt Lengkap)

P0 · Control
Baseline
max_tokens 300
You are a medical research assistant. Answer a biomedical yes/no/maybe question based solely on the provided scientific abstracts.

Context from medical literature:
{context}

Question: {question}

Instructions:
- Carefully read the context and assess whether it supports or refutes the question.
- Provide a brief explanation (2-3 sentences) using ONLY the information above.
- End your response with EXACTLY ONE of these words on its own line: yes, no, or maybe.
  - yes   : the evidence supports the hypothesis, even if not perfectly conclusive
  - no    : the evidence refutes or does not support the hypothesis
  - maybe : ONLY if the evidence is directly contradictory (some findings say yes,
            others say no), or if the context contains no relevant information at all
- IMPORTANT: If the evidence leans in one direction, even partially, choose yes or no.
  Do NOT use maybe simply because the evidence is limited or not 100% certain.

IV · Varian 2 dari 5

P1 Strict Grounding + Balanced Maybe

Tujuan

Memperbaiki bias anti-maybe di P0 dengan tetap menjaga grounding yang ketat. Definisi maybe diperjelas dengan tiga kondisi konkret.

Perbedaan vs P0

  • Aturan grounding ditulis bernomor dan eksplisit (3 aturan)
  • Maybe diberi 3 kondisi (mixed, inconclusive, no direct evidence)
  • Larangan terhadap maybe DIHAPUS

Ekspektasi hasil

  • Akurasi maybe naik signifikan (target dari 5% jadi >15%)
  • Akurasi yes/no stabil atau turun sedikit
  • Faithfulness sedikit naik karena grounding lebih eksplisit

Diuji untuk hipotesis

H1 (Anti-Maybe Bias) — apakah menghapus larangan eksplisit memperbaiki recall maybe?

Intuisi Desain
Kalau LLM "diijinkan" memilih maybe untuk kasus inconclusive, akurasi keseluruhan bisa naik karena banyak kasus di PubMedQA memang inconclusive secara legitimate.
IV · Varian 2 dari 5 (Verbatim)

P1 Strict Grounding (Prompt Lengkap)

P1 · H1
Strict Grounding + Balanced Maybe
max_tokens 300
You are a medical research assistant evaluating biomedical evidence.

GROUNDING RULES (strict):
1. Use ONLY the information in the provided abstracts below. Do NOT use any external medical knowledge, common sense, or assumptions.
2. If the abstracts do not contain enough information to answer, you MUST say so.
3. Do not infer beyond what is explicitly stated.

Context from medical literature:
{context}

Question: {question}

Decision rubric (apply in order):
- Answer "yes"   : the abstracts clearly and consistently support the claim in the question.
- Answer "no"    : the abstracts clearly refute the claim, or report no significant effect.
- Answer "maybe" : (a) findings are mixed or context-dependent (e.g. effect appears in subgroup A but not subgroup B), OR (b) the abstracts are inconclusive, OR (c) the abstracts do not directly address the question.

Response format:
- One short paragraph (2-3 sentences) citing the specific evidence from the abstracts.
- Then a final line containing only one word: yes, no, or maybe.

IV · Varian 3 dari 5

P2 Zero-shot Chain-of-Thought

Tujuan

Memaksa model menampilkan rantai reasoning eksplisit sebelum keputusan final. Meningkatkan faithfulness karena model harus menyebutkan bukti.

Struktur reasoning (4 step)

  • Step 1 Evidence: kutip temuan dari abstrak
  • Step 2 Direction: support, refute, mixed
  • Step 3 Strength: conclusive, partial, weak
  • Step 4 Decision: pilih yes, no, maybe

Ekspektasi hasil

  • Faithfulness naik (target +3-5%)
  • Label accuracy stabil atau sedikit naik
  • Step 3 (Strength) khusus didesain fix bias maybe

Trade-off

Token output naik ~70 persen (300 → 500). Biaya output cost naik proporsional.

Diuji untuk hipotesis

P2 Zero-shot CoT (Prompt Lengkap)

P2 · H2
Zero-shot Chain-of-Thought
max_tokens 500
You are a medical research assistant. Answer a biomedical yes/no/maybe question using only the provided abstracts.

Context from medical literature:
{context}

Question: {question}

Reason step by step before deciding. Use this exact structure:

Step 1 (Evidence): List the specific findings from the abstracts that are relevant to the question. Quote or paraphrase briefly.
Step 2 (Direction): Determine whether the evidence supports, refutes, or is mixed about the claim in the question.
Step 3 (Strength): Note whether the evidence is conclusive, partial, or weak.
Step 4 (Decision): Based on steps 1-3, choose yes, no, or maybe.
  - yes   if evidence consistently supports the claim
  - no    if evidence consistently refutes the claim or shows no effect
  - maybe if evidence is mixed, subgroup-dependent, inconclusive, or absent

End your response with a final line containing only one word: yes, no, or maybe.

IV · Varian 4 dari 5

P3 Few-shot Chain-of-Thought

Tujuan

Reasoning approach sama dengan P2, tapi dengan tiga demonstrasi seimbang (1 yes, 1 no, 1 maybe). Contoh "maybe" menggunakan subgroup-dependent finding.

Karakteristik demonstrasi

  • Ex1 (yes): aspirin dan recurrent MI, p<0.001
  • Ex2 (no): vitamin C dan common cold, p=0.42
  • Ex3 (maybe): statins di elderly, subgroup-dependent

Ekspektasi hasil

  • Format consistency lebih baik dari P2
  • Akurasi maybe naik paling tinggi karena contoh konkret
  • Performa keseluruhan paling stabil

Trade-off

Prompt ~3x lebih panjang dari P0. Input token cost naik (tapi cache prompt OpenAI bisa mitigate).

Diuji untuk hipotesis

H3 (Few-shot Anchoring)

Catatan
Prompt P3 panjang sehingga verbatim-nya dipecah jadi 2 slide: bagian demonstrasi dan bagian task.

P3 Few-shot CoT (Bagian Demonstrasi)

P3 · H3
Few-shot Chain-of-Thought (3 demonstrasi)
max_tokens 500
You are a medical research assistant. Answer biomedical yes/no/maybe questions using only the provided abstracts.

Here are three examples of how to reason and answer:

--- Example 1 ---
Context: [1] (RESULTS) Daily aspirin use was associated with a 22% reduction in recurrent myocardial infarction (RR 0.78, 95% CI 0.71-0.85, p<0.001) in patients with prior MI over a 5-year follow-up.
Question: Does aspirin reduce the risk of recurrent myocardial infarction?
Reasoning: The abstract directly reports a statistically significant 22% reduction in recurrent MI with aspirin. The effect is consistent and clinically meaningful.
Final answer:
yes

--- Example 2 ---
Context: [1] (RESULTS) Vitamin C supplementation (1000 mg/day) did not significantly reduce the duration or severity of common cold symptoms compared to placebo (p=0.42).
Question: Does vitamin C reduce the duration of common cold symptoms?
Reasoning: The abstract explicitly reports no significant effect of vitamin C on cold duration. The p-value (0.42) indicates no statistically significant difference.
Final answer:
no

--- Example 3 ---
Context: [1] (RESULTS) In our cohort, statin therapy reduced cardiovascular events in patients aged 50-70 (HR 0.72) but showed no significant benefit in patients over 75 (HR 0.96, 95% CI 0.81-1.14).
Question: Do statins reduce cardiovascular events in elderly patients?
Reasoning: The evidence is subgroup-dependent. Statins help patients aged 50-70, but not those over 75. Since "elderly" can include both groups, the evidence is mixed.
Final answer:
maybe

IV · Varian 5 dari 5

P4 Clinical Persona + Causal CoT

Tujuan

Diadaptasi dari MedCoT-RAG (Wang et al., 2024). Memberi persona "clinical evidence reviewer" dan memaksa workflow 4 tahap yang meniru proses diagnostik klinis.

4-Stage Workflow

  • (A) Identify Findings: study design + effect size
  • (B) Assess Quality: direct vs indirect, caveats
  • (C) Weigh Evidence: support, contradict, partial
  • (D) Final Decision: yes, no, maybe

Ekspektasi hasil

  • Faithfulness paling tinggi di antara semua varian
  • Performa optimal saat dipasangkan dengan Context Reranking

Risiko

Prompt sangat ketat sehingga model lebih sering memilih maybe (over-cautious). Gain di binary yes/no di paper asli cuma +1.2 poin.

Diuji untuk hipotesis

H4 (Sinergi Prompt-Metode) via kombinasi P4 + CR.

IV · Varian 5 dari 5 (Verbatim)

P4 Clinical Persona (Prompt Lengkap)

P4 · H4
Clinical Persona + Structured Causal CoT
max_tokens 500
You are a clinical evidence reviewer with expertise in biomedical research methodology and critical appraisal of scientific abstracts. Your role is to evaluate whether the provided scientific evidence supports a given research claim.

Context from medical literature:
{context}

Research question: {question}

Apply the following clinical reasoning workflow strictly in order:

(A) IDENTIFY FINDINGS
    List the key empirical findings from the abstracts that relate to the question. Note the study design (RCT, cohort, cross-sectional, etc.) and any reported effect sizes, confidence intervals, or p-values when available.

(B) ASSESS EVIDENCE QUALITY
    Briefly note: Is the evidence direct or indirect? Is it consistent across the abstracts? Are there subgroup differences or important caveats?

(C) WEIGH EVIDENCE AGAINST CLAIM
    Compare the findings to the specific claim in the question. Does the evidence support, contradict, or partially address the claim?

(D) FINAL DECISION
    Choose exactly one:
    - "yes"   : evidence consistently and clearly supports the claim
    - "no"    : evidence consistently refutes the claim, or reports no effect
    - "maybe" : evidence is mixed, subgroup-dependent, inconclusive, or does not directly address the claim

IMPORTANT: Base your decision only on the abstracts provided. Do not introduce outside medical knowledge.

End your response with a final line containing exactly one word: yes, no, or maybe.

Catatan Kritis tentang MedCoT-RAG

Paper MedCoT-RAG asli diuji di MedQA-US (USMLE multiple-choice), MMLU-Med, dan BioASQ-Y/N. Pada BioASQ-Y/N (binary yes/no, paling mirip PubMedQA), gain MedCoT-RAG hanya +1.2 poin absolut dibanding +5.8 poin di MedQA-US.

Implikasi: pada pertanyaan binary/ternary tertutup seperti PubMedQA, manfaat structured causal CoT mungkin terbatas. Varian P4 di TA ini menguji apakah pola ini terkonfirmasi.

Glossary Singkat

IstilahDefinisi
RAGRetrieval-Augmented Generation. Sistem yang mengambil dokumen relevan lalu menggunakannya sebagai konteks untuk LLM.
QRQuery Rewriting. Menulis ulang pertanyaan pengguna sebelum retrieval untuk meningkatkan kualitas hasil.
CRContext Reranking. Mengurutkan ulang konteks hasil retrieval menggunakan model CrossEncoder.
BM25Sparse retrieval berbasis frekuensi kata. Cocok untuk pencocokan kata kunci eksak.
RRFReciprocal Rank Fusion. Metode menggabungkan ranking dari beberapa retriever menjadi satu ranking final.
CoTChain-of-Thought. Prompting yang memaksa model menampilkan langkah reasoning sebelum jawaban akhir.
FaithfulnessSeberapa setia jawaban LLM terhadap konteks yang diberikan. Tinggi = klaim di jawaban terdukung konteks.
Context RecallSeberapa lengkap konteks mencakup informasi referensi (ground truth).
Context PrecisionSeberapa tepat konteks dalam mendukung jawaban yang benar. Tinggi = konteks tidak berisi noise.
